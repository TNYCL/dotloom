# Code of conduct

Dotloom follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1. In short:

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent; give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere. Report incidents to
the maintainer at **tunaycl@outlook.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and
promptly. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful, following
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary
ban, permanent ban).
